# Blue Cross Blue Shield of Illinois - Prior Authorization Policy Changes

**Document Type:** Policy Bulletin  
**Payer:** Blue Cross Blue Shield of Illinois (BCBSIL)  
**Announcement Date:** October 1, 2025 (Updated: November 14, 2025)  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Policy Type:** Prior Authorization Requirements  

---

## Executive Summary

Blue Cross Blue Shield of Illinois is implementing new prior authorization requirements for multiple service categories effective January 1, 2026. These changes affect Commercial, Medicare Advantage, and Community Health Plans members, with services now requiring review by Carelon Medical Benefits Management or EviCore.

---

## Affected Member Populations

### Commercial Non-HMO Members
- **Review Entity:** Carelon Medical Benefits Management
- **Impact:** New prior authorization requirements for three service categories

### Blue Cross Medicare Advantage (PPO) Members  
- **Review Entity:** EviCore
- **Impact:** New prior authorization requirements for specialty services

### Blue Cross Community Health Plans Members
- **Review Entity:** Carelon Medical Benefits Management / BCBSIL
- **Impact:** Multiple changes including new requirements and removed categories

---

## Policy Changes by Member Type

### Commercial Non-HMO Members (Carelon Review)

**NEW Prior Authorization Requirements:**

1. **Advanced Imaging Codes**
   - Status: NEW requirement effective 1/1/2026
   - Review: Carelon Medical Benefits Management
   - Note: All advanced imaging services now require pre-authorization

2. **Sleep Study Codes**
   - Status: NEW requirement effective 1/1/2026
   - Review: Carelon Medical Benefits Management
   - Note: Sleep diagnostic and treatment services now require authorization

3. **Genetic Testing Codes**
   - Status: NEW requirement effective 1/1/2026
   - Review: Carelon Medical Benefits Management
   - Note: Genetic and molecular testing services now require authorization

---

### Medicare Advantage (PPO) Members (EviCore Review)

**NEW Prior Authorization Requirements:**

1. **Specialty Drug Codes**
   - Status: NEW requirement effective 1/1/2026
   - Review: EviCore
   - Note: High-cost specialty pharmaceutical services now require authorization

2. **Molecular Genetic Laboratory Testing Codes**
   - Status: NEW requirement effective 1/1/2026
   - Review: EviCore
   - Note: Advanced genetic lab testing now requires authorization

---

### Community Health Plans Members (Carelon/BCBSIL Review)

**NEW Prior Authorization Requirements:**

1. **Specialized Radiological Codes**
   - Status: NEW requirement effective 1/1/2026
   - Review: Carelon/BCBSIL

**REMOVED Prior Authorization Requirements:**

1. **Early and Periodic Screening, Diagnostic and Treatment (EPSDT) Codes**
   - Status: Authorization requirement REMOVED effective 1/1/2026
   - Note: These preventive services for children no longer require prior authorization

**REPLACED Prior Authorization Requirements:**

1. **Specialty Oncological Drug Codes**
   - Status: Requirements REPLACED/MODIFIED effective 1/1/2026
   - Note: Cancer treatment drug authorization process has been updated

## Provider Requirements and Responsibilities

### Pre-Service Verification

**CRITICAL:** Providers must **always check eligibility and benefits FIRST** before delivering services.

**Verification Methods:**
- Availity Essentials portal
- Preferred vendor portals
- Electronic eligibility systems

**Purpose:** Confirm prior authorization requirements and identify the correct utilization management vendor (Carelon vs. EviCore)

### Authorization Submission

Providers must submit prior authorization requests through the appropriate vendor:
- **Carelon Medical Benefits Management** - for Commercial and Community Health Plans
- **EviCore** - for Medicare Advantage members

### Financial Risk Warning

⚠️ **CRITICAL COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENT:**

"Services delivered without required authorization **may be denied for payment** and the rendering provider **may not seek reimbursement from the member.**"

**Provider Risk Exposure:**
- Denied claims cannot be balanced billed to patients
- Full financial responsibility falls on the provider
- No appeals pathway for services delivered without authorization
