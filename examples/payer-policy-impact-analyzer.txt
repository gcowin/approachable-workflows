Workflow: Payer Policy Change Impact Analyzer

Purpose: Analyze healthcare payer policy changes and estimate financial impact to identify revenue risks before they affect the organization.

Start Activity: Receive Policy Change Notice

# Phase 1: Policy Change Analysis

Activity: Receive Policy Change Notice
Handles: New payer policy bulletin or update notification
Needs: Policy bulletin, payer name, effective date
Do: Extract key metadata including payer, policy type, effective date, and policy scope
Creates: Policy change summary, Key metadata record
Next: Parse Policy Document

Activity: Parse Policy Document
Needs: Policy change summary, Full policy document
Do: Read and structure the policy document into sections covering reimbursement changes, coding updates, authorization requirements, and billing rule modifications
Creates: Structured policy sections, Change categories
Verify: All major sections identified and categorized
Next: Identify Policy Changes

Activity: Identify Policy Changes
Needs: Structured policy sections, Previous policy version (if available)
Do: Compare new policy to previous version and identify specific changes in reimbursement rates, procedure codes, authorization requirements, and documentation standards
Creates: Detailed change list, Change severity ratings
Verify: Changes are specific and measurable
If Failed: Flag document for manual review
Next: Summarize Policy Impact

Activity: Summarize Policy Impact
Needs: Detailed change list, Change categories
Do: Create executive summary highlighting critical changes, effective dates, and preliminary impact areas
Creates: Executive policy summary, Critical change highlights
Next: Identify Affected Services

# Phase 2: Impact Identification

Activity: Identify Affected Services
Needs: Detailed change list, Provider service catalog
Do: Map policy changes to specific services, procedures, and CPT/HCPCS codes offered by the healthcare system
Creates: Affected services list, Procedure code mappings
Verify: All relevant services identified
Next: Map to Departments and Service Lines

Activity: Map to Departments and Service Lines
Needs: Affected services list, Organizational structure data
Do: Identify which departments, service lines, and provider groups are impacted by the policy changes
Creates: Department impact map, Service line risk assessment
Next: Retrieve Historical Data

Activity: Retrieve Historical Data
Kind: Do Together

Activity: Pull Claims Data
Needs: Affected services list, Claims database access
Do: Extract 12-24 months of claims data for affected procedure codes and service lines including claim volumes, billed amounts, and paid amounts
Creates: Historical claims dataset
Verify: Data completeness and date range coverage

Activity: Pull Denial Data
Needs: Affected services list, Denial tracking system
Do: Extract denial history for affected services including denial reasons, appeal outcomes, and write-off amounts
Creates: Historical denial dataset
Verify: Denial reasons mapped correctly

Activity: Pull Contract Data
Needs: Payer name, Contract management system
Do: Retrieve current contract terms, reimbursement schedules, and negotiated rates for the specific payer
Creates: Contract terms summary
Verify: Contract is current and applicable

End Do Together
Next: Analyze Claims Patterns

Activity: Analyze Claims Patterns
Needs: Historical claims dataset, Affected services list
Do: Calculate claim volumes, average reimbursement per service, seasonal patterns, and top revenue-generating procedures for affected services
Creates: Claims pattern analysis, Revenue baseline metrics
Verify: Calculations accurate and patterns identified
Next: Analyze Current Practice Gaps

# Phase 3: Gap and Financial Analysis

Activity: Analyze Current Practice Gaps
Needs: Detailed change list, Claims pattern analysis, Current billing procedures
Do: Compare new policy requirements against current billing practices to identify gaps in documentation, coding, authorization processes, and compliance
Creates: Gap analysis report, Non-compliance risk areas
Verify: Gaps are specific and actionable
Next: Estimate Revenue Impact

Activity: Estimate Revenue Impact
Kind: Do Together

Activity: Calculate Reimbursement Impact
Needs: Claims pattern analysis, Detailed change list, Contract terms summary
Do: Calculate projected revenue change based on new reimbursement rates, annual claim volumes, and rate differentials
Creates: Reimbursement impact estimate
Verify: Calculations use correct baseline and rates

Activity: Calculate Denial Risk Exposure
Needs: Historical denial dataset, Gap analysis report, Claims pattern analysis
Do: Estimate potential increase in denials based on new requirements and current practice gaps, including expected denial rate increase and average denial amount
Creates: Denial risk estimate
Verify: Risk assumptions documented

Activity: Calculate Authorization Impact
Needs: Detailed change list, Claims pattern analysis
Do: Estimate revenue delay or loss from new prior authorization requirements including delay costs and potential authorization denial rates
Creates: Authorization impact estimate
Verify: Authorization volume estimates reasonable

End Do Together
Next: Aggregate Financial Impact

Activity: Aggregate Financial Impact
Needs: Reimbursement impact estimate, Denial risk estimate, Authorization impact estimate
Do: Combine all financial estimates into total annual impact, categorized by impact type and confidence level
Creates: Total financial impact estimate, Impact breakdown by category, Confidence ratings
Verify: No double-counting, totals reconcile
If Failed: Recalculate individual components
Next: Identify High-Risk Areas

Activity: Identify High-Risk Areas
Needs: Total financial impact estimate, Department impact map, Service line risk assessment
Do: Rank service lines and departments by financial exposure to prioritize mitigation efforts
Creates: Risk-ranked service lines, Priority mitigation targets
Next: Develop Mitigation Strategies

# Phase 4: Recommendation Development

Activity: Develop Mitigation Strategies
Needs: Gap analysis report, Risk-ranked service lines, Detailed change list
Do: Create specific action plans for each high-risk area including process changes, documentation improvements, staff training needs, and timeline for implementation
Creates: Mitigation action plan, Implementation timeline
Verify: Actions are specific and assignable
Next: Estimate Mitigation Costs

Activity: Estimate Mitigation Costs
Needs: Mitigation action plan
Do: Calculate costs for implementing mitigation strategies including training, process changes, technology updates, and staffing adjustments
Creates: Mitigation cost estimate, Cost-benefit analysis
Verify: Cost estimates include all implementation components
Next: Develop Monitoring Plan

Activity: Develop Monitoring Plan
Needs: Risk-ranked service lines, Mitigation action plan
Do: Define key performance indicators, monitoring frequency, reporting structure, and early warning triggers for tracking policy change impact
Creates: Monitoring and reporting plan, KPI definitions
Next: Compile Executive Report

Activity: Compile Executive Report
Needs: Executive policy summary, Total financial impact estimate, Department impact map, Mitigation action plan, Mitigation cost estimate, Monitoring and reporting plan
Do: Create comprehensive executive report with policy summary, financial impact, affected areas, recommendations, costs, and implementation timeline
Creates: Executive impact report, Presentation deck
Verify: Report is clear, complete, and actionable
Next: Submit for Review

# Phase 5: Review and Approval

Activity: Submit for Review
Needs: Executive impact report
Do: Distribute report to Revenue Cycle Leadership, Finance Department, and Executive Leadership for review with review deadline and approval requirements
Creates: Review package with supporting documentation
Next: Leadership Review

Activity: Leadership Review
Needs: Executive impact report, All supporting analysis
Do: Finance and Revenue Cycle leadership review financial impact estimates, mitigation strategies, and cost-benefit analysis, then approve or request modifications
Creates: Review decision, Feedback for revisions
Choice:
  If: Approved
    Next: Distribute Final Report
  If: Revisions Requested
    Next: Incorporate Feedback
  If: Rejected
    Next: Document Decision and Archive

Activity: Incorporate Feedback
Needs: Review decision, Feedback for revisions, Executive impact report
Do: Update analysis and recommendations based on leadership feedback and recalculate impacts if assumptions changed
Creates: Revised executive report
Next: Submit for Review

Activity: Distribute Final Report
Needs: Executive impact report (approved), Review decision
Do: Distribute approved report to all stakeholders including department heads, contracting team, and implementation leads
Creates: Stakeholder notification, Implementation authorization
Next: Initiate Implementation Tracking

Activity: Initiate Implementation Tracking
Needs: Mitigation action plan (approved), Monitoring and reporting plan
Do: Set up tracking systems, assign action items to responsible parties, establish review cadence, and create monitoring dashboard
Creates: Implementation tracker, Assigned action items, Monitoring dashboard
Next: End

Activity: Document Decision and Archive
Needs: Review decision, Executive impact report
Do: Document reasons for rejection, archive analysis for future reference, and notify stakeholders
Creates: Decision documentation, Archived analysis record
Next: End
